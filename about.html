My story

From product work to enterprise delivery.

I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering in April 2023 and joined Snovasys the following month. I started on TimeChamp, contributing to leave management, TaskTimer, security, and scalability work. I also rebuilt its marketing site with performance and search visibility goals.

In January 2025, I moved to the AICPA & CIMA client assignment. Alongside production features and issue resolution, I independently architected and developed Mercury. I also contributed to Snovasys internal initiatives, including BIXO’s AI product plan and architecture.

I value clear requirements, dependable delivery, and systems that remain understandable after their first release. I have led onboarding support, interviewed candidates, and worked directly with stakeholders.
